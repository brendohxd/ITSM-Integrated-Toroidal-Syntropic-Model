## Maintenance

When work is planned, give it a stable ID or existing parent ID, then record
its owner, priority, effort, risk, dependencies, acceptance test and next
action. When work finishes or changes direction, update its row and append a
dated log entry with the result, evidence, what it contributed, and any
remaining dependent work. Record actual effort when measured. Keep retired
or superseded IDs with their reason and successor. A scoped negative result
can complete a deliverable and still hold its parent route.

Separate document reconciliation, completed calculation, physical clearance
and publication status. Apply the
[deferred Rule-9 policy](Theory/Core/ITSM_RULE9_DEFERRED_REVIEW_POLICY.md)
and the [unfinished-work protocol](Theory/Core/ITSM_UNFINISHED_WORK_COMPLETION_PROTOCOL_2026-11-05.md).
Maintain the short handoff when the order changes. This tracker carries
navigation and task status. Scientific authority stays with the owning files.
