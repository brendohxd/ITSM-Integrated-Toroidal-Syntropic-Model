Identity & boundaries

What the programme is for — and what it will not pretend

Pillars we keep

  • Open thermodynamics — entropy / syntropy dual, not a closed steam engine
  • Toroidal / compact flat topology as a serious research preference
  • Fluid plenum / continuous wake before collisionless halo particles
  • Winding–resonance structure (principle open; broken packaging rejected)
  • GR tensor sector under minimal-coupling constraints

Pillars are identity, not automatic Wilson coefficients.

Not live as Derived claims

  • Topology-alone derivation of \(a_0\) as completed quantization
  • Free-field Casimir attractor \(13/12\) → parameter-free \(H_0=72.97\)
  • Projector \(2/3\) as proven Wilson \(C_{\mathrm{obs}}\)
  • SPARC \(p=0.62\) / SPARC as independent cosmic \(H_0\)
  • “Zero free theoretical parameters / all tensions resolved”
