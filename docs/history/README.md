# Historical claims and deprecations

This page guides readers from earlier ITSM claims to their recovery
dispositions. Current scientific authority remains the
[Master Research Plan](../../Theory/Core/ITSM_Master_Research_Plan.md),
[live dashboard](../../active_research.md),
[claim migration ledger](../../Theory/Core/ITSM_Claim_Migration_Ledger.csv) and owning
gate decisions. The tables below summarize existing restrictions; they do
not change claim labels or clear a gate.

## Withdrawn formulations and open questions

| Historical formulation | Recovery disposition and remaining question |
|---|---|
| Circulation or topology derives $a_0=cH_0/(2\pi)$ | That derivation is withdrawn. Present-epoch scale matching is a named phenomenological postulate; an action-derived acceleration coefficient remains **Open**. |
| A projector trace fixes the observable coupling $C_{\rm obs}=2/3$ | The generic trace identity does not match the normalized matter vertex. The matching problem remains open under MAT-001. |
| Free Casimir stress gives a persistent $13/12$ expansion attractor and parameter-free $H_0=72.97$ | The persistent-attractor claim is **Rejected** for the tested free-scalar backreaction model. Shape-dependent stress and transient crossings are bounded results; a separately derived driven completion remains **Open**. |
| SPARC $p=0.62$, optimizer output labelled MCMC, or SPARC as an independent cosmic $H_0$ measurement | The historical validation package is rejected/quarantined. Comparator optimization and statistical methods controls do not establish a closed STAT gate or ITSM prediction. |
| A fixed NANOGrav frequency interval is an ITSM-derived prediction | The historical packaging is withdrawn. A physical spectrum requires independently supplied field, scale, sound speed, state and matching under VOR/PERT gates. |
| Automatic Solar-System compliance or a quantitative Bullet Cluster wake | These claims lack completed screening, metric/lensing and dynamical-wake derivations. The associated SCR/LEN/WAK research questions remain **Open**. |
| JWST spectral suppression proves an ITSM bottom-light IMF | The historical derivation is withdrawn. Dimensionally valid star-formation and fragmentation research remains **Open** under ASTRO-001. |
| Zero free theoretical parameters or automatic resolution of all cosmological tensions | This packaging is prohibited. Boundary conditions, unmatched coefficients, incomplete sectors and unsuccessful tests remain part of the research record. |

For exact restrictions and evidence, read the master plan's Bucket A/B/C
dispositions, the
[Ban List Reassessment and Frontier Policy](../../Theory/Core/ITSM_Ban_List_Reassessment_and_Frontier_Policy.md)
and the
[Selective Publishing Plan](../../papers/Selective-Publishing-Plan/ITSM_Selective_Publishing_Plan.md).
Rejected packaging does not make every underlying research topic forbidden.
A topic can return through a new derivation and owning gate; its old wording
supplies no support.
