What ITSM hopes to achieve

Per the Master Research Plan: ITSM is a research-stage layered effective framework — not a finished theory of everything. Turn a clear physical identity into mechanisms that earn predictions gate by gate. Untested routes stay Open.

Physical proposition

The observable vacuum is treated as an active finite-density condensate (a plenum) whose low-energy excitations, global circulation and winding, compact flat topology (especially \(T^3\) or twisted flat spaces), and exchanges with matter and a reservoir may have gravitational consequences.

That is an identity statement. Whether it becomes a competitive multi-scale cosmology depends on closed field-sector gates — not on packaging alone.

Relative to standard frameworks

Context ITSM intent Honesty bound
ΛCDM / particle DM Prefer continuous plenum / wake response over collisionless halo particles as the first account of galactic “missing mass.” Cosmology still needs a consistent excitation budget for BBN, expansion history, and structure — not pure baryons alone.
MOND / AQUAL-like laws Recover a deep-MOND structure as an IR effective law when earned; treat \(C_{\mathrm{obs}}\sim 1\) as a fair Conditional baseline for testing. Do not replace the whole stack with pure MOND.
Open thermodynamics Observable cosmos as open subsystem with entropy exhaust and syntropy intake; total system conserved. Needs constitutive \(Q_{\mathrm{syn}}\) — not slogan-level “creation rates.”
Superfluid / condensate analogues Keep finite-density order parameter, phonons, winding, defects as ontology. Separations: UV condensate ≠ IR force; no automatic cycle-count coefficients.

Founding pillars & Active Research Paths

Candidate mechanisms are tracked through the formal research gate pipeline (Master Plan §2.2). Their current labels are deliberately narrower than the programme's aims:

  • Matter matching and unitarity — MAT-001 is blocked and UVIR-003 remains in progress. The scale-compensator fork does not derive numeric K_Q, V, a physical amplitude or an EFT cutoff. · Open / Blocked
  • Gravitational lensing & SPARC kinematics — LEN-001 remains open downstream of matter matching. DISK-001 is a conditional methods package; STAT-001 is not a closed gate. · Open / Methods Only
  • Winding & acoustic modes — Compact-box Bogoliubov and winding calculations remain scaffolds pending macroscopic circulation matching and a detector response. · Open Scaffold
  • Toroidal moduli dynamics — Free Casimir dilution gives a scoped negative result for persistent anisotropy. TOP-X4 Plan 11 has bounded static and finite-charge operator checkpoints, but the dynamic state, stress and physical Hessian are open. · Dynamic Hold
  • Fluid plenum & causal wake — The current damped scalar-field control uses prescribed sources; hydrodynamics, shocks, matter coupling and metric lensing remain open. · Exploratory Control
  • Open thermodynamics — A single-mode inserted-rate GKSL steady-state control exists; no microscopic reservoir Hamiltonian, covariant stress or constitutive Qμ has been derived. · Phenomenological Control
  • Cosmological perturbations & S₈ — The current executable result is an unmodified ΛCDM calibration. No ITSM Boltzmann hierarchy or S₈ prediction has been computed. · Calibration / Open

What the recovery programme has established

  • Separated the condensate, preferred frame, infrared force, matter, reservoir, topology and optional wake sectors so their coefficients cannot be silently identified.
  • Rejected the historical trace-ratio derivation of 2/3 and kept a₀, K_Q and V outside the Derived bucket.
  • Reclassified SPARC work as methods-only and quarantined optimizer output that had been described as posterior inference.
  • Established the scoped free-Casimir dilution result and recorded why a prescribed driven ratio is not a stationary attractor.
  • Built reproducible, checksum-tracked checkpoints whose script-level passes do not automatically promote physics or publication claims.
