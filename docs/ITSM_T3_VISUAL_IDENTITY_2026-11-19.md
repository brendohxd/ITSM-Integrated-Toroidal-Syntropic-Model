# ITSM T³ visual identity — 19 November 2026

## Purpose

Represent the proposed three-dimensional compact spatial topology accurately,
and give the accumulated research a deliberate, readable public presentation.
The design uses an illustrative fundamental cell with opposite faces identified
by translation in three independent spatial directions. Time remains nonperiodic.

## Public surfaces

- Seven pages share `atlas.css`, the periodic mark, six-item navigation and a
  restrained charcoal, ivory, teal, amber and violet palette.
- The homepage replaces active doughnut artwork with a purpose-built T³ cell.
- The model page adds an accessible coordinate explorer: x, y and z, an explicit
  boundary identification, keyboard-native range controls and a static fallback.
- Resources provides attributed SVG/PNG downloads for the cell and paired-face
  guide. A matching social image and favicon/app icon family complete the identity.
- `Scripts/site/build_t3_visuals_v1.py` builds the assets with PyMuPDF and Pillow.
  It preserves preimages of any replaced active icon. Earlier artwork is retained.

## Scientific interpretation

The diagrams specify identifications, not an external box, a physical wall,
an observed topology, a preferred cell shape or a measured compactification scale.
They do not establish an acceleration coefficient, force law or observational
validation. A doughnut surface is T²; local circulation and global T³ topology
remain distinct. No manuscript or scientific evidence is revised by this design.
