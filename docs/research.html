The current research route

Dust closure → physical validity and GR recovery → numerical weak-field prediction.

  1. 1
    Bounded research complete

    A controlled, short-time dust calculation

    The registered linear calculation now retains the evolving basis, constraints, momentum drift and fast-mode forcing. Its first finite-wavenumber correction has explicit remainder bounds and an established operator convergence order.

    Certified numerical error control covers all five registered modes on the dimensionless interval t ∈ [0, 0.05], preserving the nonmonotonic complementary responses. This is not full nonlinear dust dynamics, observational accuracy or a physical stability certificate.

    Physical assessment incomplete

    A validity window and a justified GR limit

    Three parameter families have been surveyed under the same conditional R4C1 action. Some sampled nonzero-mode kinetic checks are positive, but mean modes, singular endpoints, coupled interactions and the physical frequency/momentum domain remain unresolved.

    The latest fixed quadratic-charge preparation does not remove the registered retarded-response timing warning; the coefficient is larger at all nine sampled family/parameter points. That is a bounded warning, not a physical cutoff or a theorem against every possible ITSM action.

    Numerical prediction not yet entered

    Fix the matter coupling, then predict the force

    A genuine prediction needs independent information fixing both the matter residue and the nonlinear force normalization. Calculating a residue from assumed coefficients does not determine those coefficients.

    No target-independent numerical acceleration scale has been sealed. The next observational fit cannot supply the missing derivation by choosing parameters to obtain the desired answer.

What the new mechanism screens tell us

13 October · separate declared controls, not a new accepted parent theory.

RM1 · Radial mass portal

A specific route fails

The assumed matter-mass portal provides a radial source, but not the required nonzero static coupling to the gapless phase mode. That mechanism is rejected for this particular purpose.

ST1 · Induced-scale control

A relation, not a selected number

A scalar–tensor control can provide a parameterized static source. Its kinetic ratio and matter-mass assignment remain inputs; a same-scale mass choice cancels the direct source.

ST2 · Heavy-field threshold

A calculable correction, with open inputs

A declared heavy field supplies a finite quantum correction. Renormalized boundary inputs and a viable physical background are still needed; the displayed vacuum potential has a stationarity obstruction.

What comes next?

The approved continuation keeps the R4C1 action unchanged and investigates coupled interactions and a controlled physical window. A usable result must address the constraints and energy, the zero mode and singular domains, nonlinear error control, and a nonempty admissible band.

In parallel, matching work can investigate the missing physical inputs without declaring a numerical prediction. If the action cannot earn the required domain, that calls for an explicit action-selection decision—not a silent change of model.

The wider programme

Galaxy rotation, lensing and clusters are eventual cross-scale tests. Expansion, structure growth, primordial abundances, gravitational waves and high-redshift galaxies need the appropriate background and perturbation equations first.

TOP-X4 remains a separate quantum-completion lane. Its bounded operator and state work does not yet supply the missing stress, gravity/ghost, parity, counterterm and physical-Hessian sectors. It has not replaced the current action route.
