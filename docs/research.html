External evidence watch

14 September 2026 · unreviewed inputs, no gate change

Six preprints survived duplicate and relevance screening. They define bounded future tests and do not constitute Rule-9 review, a physics-gate receipt or publication clearance. The BBN analyses are the highest- priority additions.

BBN nuisance marginalization

Akharman et al. · arXiv:2609.12065 presents Planck+DESI DR2 BAO+BBN and BAO+BBN+CMB inference with explicit BBN nuisance propagation; its fiducial h remains near 0.68.

ITSM test: Couple the early plenum and Qμ to PArthENoPE/AlterBBN, marginalize nuclear and abundance inputs, and report abundances, rs and H0.

Empirical primordial helium

Loverde, Saravanan & Weiner · arXiv:2609.13140 uses LBT helium to test early-time radiation, relic and varying-constant scenarios without enforcing standard-BBN closure.

ITSM test: Derive Yp/D/H versus plenum density, Geff(z), Qμ and expansion; fit helium independently and predeclare the z≈109 plenum bound.

JWST clustering targets

Lim et al. · arXiv:2609.12040 supplies clustering-derived halo-mass and bias targets for JADES through high redshift, with field-variance and star-formation-model limits.

ITSM test: Generate JADES-matched light cones and report Mh, bh, cosmic variance and assembly-bias sensitivity against ΛCDM and bursty controls.

Bright and dark sirens

Colombo, Triscari-Benimavò & Mastrogiovanni · arXiv:2609.12053 finds that a small bright-siren sample helps break the H0–Ωm degeneracy affecting dark sirens.

ITSM test: Infer H0 and Ωm jointly within ITSM before propagating into a0=CχcH0; do not import a ΛCDM prior.

Primordial curvature limits

An et al. · arXiv:2609.12867 reports no tested scalar-induced LVK background and limits primordial curvature even where PBHs are rare or evaporated.

ITSM test: Apply the O1–O4a likelihood only if ITSM derives an enhanced scalar spectrum or induced ΩGW(f); keep it separate from late-time propagation tests.

Intrinsic alignments in lensing

Chawla et al. · arXiv:2609.12038 shows in Stage-IV simulations that unmodelled intrinsic alignments can bias parameters and mimic lower Ωm/S8.

ITSM test: Add alignment fields to weak-lensing mocks and jointly fit spectra, PDFs, peaks, minima and voids before attributing low S8 to plenum dynamics.
