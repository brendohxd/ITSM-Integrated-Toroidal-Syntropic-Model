Latest research · 4 November 2026

Keeping the coupled perturbations under control

A viable model must control the disturbances its own equations permit. The latest calculation retains density, both zero-speed directions and the fast force pair. Its initial principal calculation now includes all 20 coupled directions, with a positive ordinary energy and a periodic change of variables that preserves means without a Fourier cutoff.

This work concerns R-tau, an unadopted comparison action in which the regulator uses the existing dust-clock normal while the independent frame remains. It has not replaced the original R4C1 action or selected the wider ITSM proposal's final equations.

What is established within scope: a complete initial density-source estimate, an exact action identity supporting the ordinary principal energy, both ordinary/force coupling blocks, and a bounded variable-coefficient composition for the registered periodic principal operator. The separate homogeneous benchmark's earlier t = 0.05 estimate remains a separate result.

What remains open: the full actual generator, moving constraints, time-dependent energy and a preserved background domain. The principal calculation is useful progress toward that proof; it does not establish full dynamical stability, an interacting physical window, healthy recovery of general relativity or independently fixed matter/force matching.

A separate observational comparator

Full-SPARC sampling, with unresolved cases kept visible

All 175 galaxies and 3,391 radial measurements were included, allowing uncertainty in distance, disk inclination and stellar mass-to-light ratios. After targeted continuation of the initial 54 sampling failures, 157 of 175 meet the declared convergence diagnostics. 18 remain unresolved. The original passing cases and failed attempts are preserved.

The fit uses a fixed empirical acceleration law, the simple-nu comparator. These diagnostics assess sampling behaviour; they do not establish an adequate physical fit, an action-derived ITSM prediction, a new acceleration scale or preference over competing models. The next step is to diagnose the remaining cases and test assumptions and predictions.

What the new mechanism screens tell us

13 October · separate declared controls, not a new accepted parent theory.

RM1 · Radial mass portal

A specific route fails

The assumed matter-mass portal provides a radial source, but not the required nonzero static coupling to the gapless phase mode. That mechanism is rejected for this particular purpose.

ST1 · Induced-scale control

A relation, not a selected number

A scalar–tensor control can provide a parameterized static source. Its kinetic ratio and matter-mass assignment remain inputs; a same-scale mass choice cancels the direct source.

ST2 · Heavy-field threshold

A calculable correction, with open inputs

A declared heavy field supplies a finite quantum correction. Renormalized boundary inputs and a viable physical background are still needed; the displayed vacuum potential has a stationarity obstruction.

What comes next?

The next bounded step is the actual R-tau generator remainder, time-dependent energy and evolving constraint domain. The comparator remains unadopted; the original R4C1 evidence is preserved. Only supported evolution can lead to a coupled interaction window and scoped general-relativity tests. Missing matching inputs remain a separate obligation.

In parallel, matching work can investigate the missing physical inputs without declaring a numerical prediction. If the action cannot earn the required domain, that calls for an explicit action-selection decision—not a silent change of model.

The wider programme

Galaxy rotation, lensing and clusters are eventual cross-scale tests. Expansion, structure growth, primordial abundances, gravitational waves and high-redshift galaxies need the appropriate background and perturbation equations first.

TOP-X4 remains a separate quantum-completion lane. Its bounded operator and state work does not yet supply the missing stress, gravity/ghost, parity, counterterm and physical-Hessian sectors. It has not replaced the current action route.
