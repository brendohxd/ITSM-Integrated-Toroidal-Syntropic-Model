Claim hygiene

The ITSM programme treats intellectual honesty and mathematical rigor as load-bearing. Status labels keep track of open research routes and rigorous derivations.

Status of every claim

LabelMeaning
Derived Checked calculation from declared assumptions in a stated domain

Open Defined calculation or test incomplete — default for untested routes
Rejected Stated derivation form failed; may return only with a new gate and new math — not by slogan
