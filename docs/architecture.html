Layered effective architecture

Architecture is an inventory of sectors and separations — not a claim that every coefficient is already derived. Full detail lives in the recovery architecture document on GitHub.

Schematic action

Complete schematic (architecture, not closed microphysics): 

S = S_EH + S_Φ + S_U + S_align + S_ψ + S_m + S_R + S_int + S_W
SectorRole
Metric \(g_{\mu\nu}\)Einstein–Hilbert baseline
Condensate \(\Phi=\rho e^{i\Theta}/\sqrt{2}\)Density, phase, winding, defects

Non-negotiable separations

Physics

  • UV condensate ≠ IR phonon / force sector
  • \(Q_{\mathrm{mp}}\) (local matter–plenum) ≠ \(Q_{\mathrm{syn}}\) (reservoir throughput)
  • Energy throughput ≠ anisotropic pressure without constitutive law

Numbers

  • Topology ≠ numerical coefficients (\(2/3\), \(13/12\))
  • Stress-energy exchange ≠ condensate charge transfer without a declared link
  • Observational claims never outrank upstream field sectors

Why this matters

Much historical damage came from collapsing these layers — counting dimensions or cycles as if they were Wilson coefficients, or treating a free Casimir calculation as a finished Hubble theory. Architecture is the firewall that keeps identity intact while mechanisms are rebuilt.
