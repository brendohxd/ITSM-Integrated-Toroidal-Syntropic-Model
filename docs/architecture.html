How the pieces fit

A physical proposal needs more than a name. These are the ingredients ITSM investigates, the connections it must calculate, and the assumptions it must test.

The physical ingredients

Connected, but not interchangeable.

The plenum

A vacuum with density and phase

The proposed condensate has amplitude and phase variables. Small disturbances, circulation and winding are different types of behaviour; none automatically supplies a force law for ordinary matter.

Matter & gravity

An interaction that must be derived

The model needs an explicit coupling to matter and a dynamical metric. That coupling must determine motion, exchange and light propagation consistently—not merely add an acceleration to a rotation curve.

Global space

Three periodic directions

The proposed T³ topology can be represented by a box with opposite faces identified in each spatial direction. This changes global boundary conditions and permitted modes, while allowing locally flat spatial geometry.

A boundary-condition representation, not an embedded doughnut: topology alone fixes neither an acceleration coefficient nor an expansion rate, and is not observationally established here.

The environment

An open subsystem, a conserved whole

Matter–plenum exchange and exchange with a reservoir are distinct mechanisms. Both need explicit equations and energy accounting. An exchange current by itself does not demonstrate accelerated expansion or thermodynamic stability.

From ingredients to an observable

Three dependent steps—not a shortcut from topology to a number.

  1. 1

    Specify the dynamics

    Declare the fields, action, interactions, background and boundary conditions. Derive their equations and conservation laws.

  2. 2

    Establish where they are physical

    Reduce the constraints, test stability and propagation, and determine an admissible frequency and momentum window.

  3. 3

    Predict before comparing

    Fix the required matching inputs independently, compute matter and metric responses, then confront observations with the same model.

Technical detail, when you need it

The layered effective architecture keeps the metric, condensate, preferred frame, infrared force mediator, matter, reservoir and optional wake sectors separate. Aligning two sectors does not make their coefficients identical.

S = S_EH + S_Φ + S_U + S_align + S_ψ + S_m + S_R + S_int + S_W

This is an inventory of sectors, not a completed microscopic theory. The condensate is written Φ = (ρ/√2)eiΘ; the preferred-frame field Uμ and infrared mediator ψ are independent ingredients.

The current R4C1 continuation is a declared conditional action used for bounded diagnostics. TOP-X4 is a separate queued route. Neither has supplied the missing canonical matter normalization or a completed physical weak-field prediction.
