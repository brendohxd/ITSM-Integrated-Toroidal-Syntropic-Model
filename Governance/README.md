# Governance and methodology

This guide explains the research practice used by ITSM. The physical
proposal is described in the [project introduction](../README.md) and
[Core Identity Briefing](../Theory/Core/ITSM_CORE_IDENTITY_BRIEFING.md).
The method can be examined separately from whether any particular
cosmological mechanism survives its tests.

This is a reader guide to existing rules. Binding documents keep their
current locations and authority.

## Scientific authority

The [Master Research Plan](../Theory/Core/ITSM_Master_Research_Plan.md)
sets the authority order: that plan, the
[live gate dashboard](../active_research.md) and Core Identity come first,
followed by the
[claim migration ledger](../Theory/Core/ITSM_Claim_Migration_Ledger.csv),
owning gate reports and the
[Selective Publishing Plan](../papers/Selective-Publishing-Plan/ITSM_Selective_Publishing_Plan.md).
An introduction, manuscript, website or historical archive cannot clear a
gate whose owning checklist remains incomplete.

The [Master Test Programme](../Theory/Core/ITSM_MASTER_TEST_PROGRAMME_2026-09-24.md)
orders research work. The
[execution queue](../Theory/Core/ITSM_Recovery_Execution_Queue.md) records
dated continuations within that programme.

## Claims and evidence

| Claim label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Derived** | A checked calculation from declared assumptions in a stated domain |
| **Conditional** | Requires a named postulate, matching hypothesis or restricted limit |
| **Open** | A defined calculation or test remains incomplete |
| **Rejected** | The stated derivation or implementation failed and supplies no live support under that form |

A claim names its assumptions, domain, upstream dependencies and actual
calculation. A script pass, matching checksum or model consensus establishes
only its declared scope. It cannot supply missing physics, change a failed
parent checklist or promote a conditional comparator to a prediction.

A rejection applies to the stated form and its dependent uses. The
[frontier policy](../Theory/Core/ITSM_Ban_List_Reassessment_and_Frontier_Policy.md)
and [deprecation guide](../docs/history/README.md) distinguish
withdrawn packaging from underlying questions that may remain open.

## Research execution and deferred review

The operator's
[25 September Rule-9 policy](../Theory/Core/ITSM_RULE9_DEFERRED_REVIEW_POLICY.md)
defers outstanding reviews. A locally checked result can support further
provisional work in its declared scope when review is the only unmet
requirement. The
[deferred-review register](../Theory/Verification/ITSM_RULE9_DEFERRED_REVIEW_REGISTER.md)
records evidence hashes, scope, substantive blockers and inherited review
dependencies.

Scientific failures, absent required inputs and unresolved substantive
objections hold the uses that depend on them. Diagnostic and independent
work may continue within the approved scope. Final gate closure, new
canonical **Derived** promotion and publication readiness still require the
scientific checklist and applicable review. When review resumes, the
mathematical/dimensional, numerical/pipeline and claim/gate roles follow
the mandates and evidence requirements in [GEMINI.md](../GEMINI.md).
Deferred review is not completed review.

## Reproducibility and working practice

Read the [Core Identity Briefing](../Theory/Core/ITSM_CORE_IDENTITY_BRIEFING.md),
[GEMINI.md](../GEMINI.md), [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) and the owning report before
scientific work. The master plan's rule is:

> Identity first → derive mechanisms second → restore predictions only afterward.

Use the report's focused reproduction command and the existing reproducible
environment; retain failed attempts, negative controls and original
versioned artifacts. Report the operations actually performed, check
dimensions and dependency compatibility, and preserve substantive holds.
The broad legacy runner does not establish recovery status.

The [context optimizer](../docs/ITSM_CONTEXT_OPTIMIZER.md) provides bounded
local reads and receipt views while retaining failures, unknowns and holds.
Its private logs are local working aids. No context reduction grants extra
execution authority.

Update scientific status only with the required evidence in the ledger,
owning report and manuscript. Freeze new manuscript versions rather than
overwriting releases. Publication requirements are recorded in the
[Selective Publishing Plan](../papers/Selective-Publishing-Plan/ITSM_Selective_Publishing_Plan.md).
Commit, push, publication and provider dispatch retain their own authority.
