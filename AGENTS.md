  diagnosing or claiming verification. It grants no extra command authority.

  diagnostic outputs and patch operations do not need wrapping. Do not
  repeatedly reload already-read unchanged documents; verify their hashes.

  native tool schemas, or guarantee billed-token savings. Never claim it does.
