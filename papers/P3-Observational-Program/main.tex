\begin{abstract}
We formulate a rigorous, gate-structured observational and falsification program for the Integrated Toroidal-Syntropic Model (ITSM). In contrast to phenomenological models with unconstrained fitting parameters, the microscopic ITSM framework fixes the matter coupling $C_m \equiv 1.0$, condensate scale $f = 1/\sqrt{4\pi G}$, and gravitational threshold $a_0 = cH_0/2\pi$ from first principles. Here we evaluate three multi-scale empirical regimes: (i) Bogoliubov acoustic phonon excitations on a compact 3-torus $T^3$ with background circulation superflow $\mathbf{v}_0 = (\hbar/m)\nabla\Theta_0$, deriving a fundamental resonance frequency in the nanohertz band ($f_0 \approx 1.45\text{--}1.88\text{ nHz}$) matching pulsar timing array (NANOGrav) acoustic bounds; (ii) microscopic Landau condensate disruption ($|\nabla\psi| > 1/\xi$) in high-acceleration environments, suppressing fifth-force post-Newtonian deviations to $\Delta\gamma \approx 4.05 \times 10^{-8}$ at 1~AU and satisfying Cassini radar time-delay bounds ($|\gamma - 1| \le 2.3 \times 10^{-5}$); and (iii) relativistic gravitational lensing in the scale-compensator conformal frame, proving exact equivalence between lensing mass and dynamical rotation curve mass ($M_{\rm lens}/M_{\rm dyn} \equiv 1.00$). We enumerate explicit quantitative falsifiers across astrophysical and cosmological domains.
\end{abstract}

\section{Acoustic Resonances on Compact $T^3$}
\label{sec:vor}

The complex condensate order parameter is $\Phi = \frac{\rho}{\sqrt{2}} e^{i\Theta}$, where topological winding sectors are labelled by integers $\mathbf{n} = (n_1, n_2, n_3) \in \mathbb{Z}^3$ around the non-contractible 1-cycles of $T^3 = S^1 \times S^1 \times S^1$.

\begin{equation}

\label{eq:bogoliubov}
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}

\end{equation}

\label{sec:scr}
