Dear Editor / arXiv moderators,

Please find attached a short technical note on present-epoch scale matching
between the MOND acceleration a0 and c H0, together with the weak-field
coefficient C_obs in an AQUAL-type action.

The manuscript does not claim a resolution of the missing-mass problem, a new
complete cosmology, or a verdict on MOND as a class. Its purpose is to separate
postulates from derivations for an explicitly author-defined geometric hypothesis that
combines:

  (1) a cubic flat T^3 of side L = c/H0 with a putative circulation quantum
      kappa = c L, and
  (2) the isotropic projector ratio Tr(h)/Tr(gamma) = 2/3 with the deep-MOND
      coefficient C_obs.

The argument follows circulation, local field theory, cosmology, and conditional
normalization, stating each sector's reconstruction requirements before its
consistency test. The introduction explains why the construction would link
galactic normalization to global geometry if its dynamical steps could be supplied.

The analysis finds that (1) is not circulation quantization and is kinematically
inconsistent when omega = 2 pi v/L is the cycle clock.
No centripetal acceleration is assigned to a flat torus geodesic.
The projector identity (2) does not appear in the weak-field action and cannot fix C_obs; we
derive the field-rescaling invariant C_obs = C_m^{3/2}/sqrt(C_IR). If a0 is
also free, the spherical force identifies only C_obs^2 a0. Exact dynamical
tracking L_phys(t) = c/H(t) is incompatible with fixed comoving moduli in
ordinary FLRW except on the coasting branch. The proposed cubic scale also
fails the cited Planck topology benchmark under its stated background and
statistics. In the
conditional static spherical baseline, imposing both a0 = c H0/(2 pi) and the
un-derived comparator C_obs = 2/3 gives a 4/9 effective normalization and an
approximately 18% asymptotic velocity reduction relative to C_obs = 1 at the
same input a0 and baryonic mass. The distinct comparison against the quoted
empirical acceleration scale gives about 20.4% for illustrative H0 = 70.
Neither is a measured discrepancy or a completed data fit. The manuscript
ends at analytic normalization; nuisance-marginalized galaxy inference belongs
to follow-up work, not this note.

The note is intended as a clarifying contribution to the MOND / compact-topology
literature through transferable normalization and compact-source consistency
checks. No priority claim for the coefficient identity, new universal law,
or independently fixed MOND prediction is made. The author
used AI-assisted tools for editorial drafting, source-code assistance, and
symbolic/numerical consistency checks. Final author verification of the
content, sources, and artifacts and confirmation of responsibility remain
pending. This letter must not be sent until those steps and applicable review
are completed and the author explicitly approves submission.
