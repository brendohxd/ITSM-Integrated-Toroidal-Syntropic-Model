Dear Editor / arXiv moderators,

Please find attached a short technical note on present-epoch scale matching
between the MOND acceleration a0 and c H0, together with the weak-field
coefficient C_obs in an AQUAL-type action.

The manuscript does not claim a resolution of the missing-mass problem or a
new complete cosmology. Its purpose is to separate postulates from derivations
for a class of recent geometric proposals that combine:

  (1) a cubic flat T^3 of side L = c/H0 with a putative circulation quantum
      kappa = c L, and
  (2) the isotropic projector ratio Tr(h)/Tr(gamma) = 2/3 with the deep-MOND
      coefficient C_obs.

We show that (1) is not circulation quantization, is kinematically inconsistent
in its usual (L, Gamma, omega) packaging, and is in tension with Planck cubic
topology bounds. Exact dynamical tracking L_phys(t) = c/H(t) is incompatible
with fixed comoving moduli in ordinary FLRW. The projector identity (2) does
not appear in the weak-field action and cannot fix C_obs; we derive the unique
field-rescaling invariant C_obs = C_m^{3/2}/sqrt(C_IR). Imposing both
a0 = c H0/(2 pi) and C_obs = 2/3 conflicts with the empirical RAR normalization.

The note is intended as a clarifying contribution to the MOND / compact-topology
literature and as a checklist for any future geometric derivation.
