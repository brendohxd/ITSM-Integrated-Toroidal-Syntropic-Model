Dear Editor / arXiv moderators,

Please find attached a theory paper studying a fully declared conditional
finite-density exchange action. Direct variation yields explicit matter,
plenum, and reservoir transfer currents whose on-shell balances sum to total
conservation. A formal reduced dust-density variable has the standard positive
uncoupled kinetic normalization, independently matched to a coupled-fluid EFT.

The manuscript also reports the limits of the construction. Homogeneous
evolution does not identify the cubic spatial-force coefficient; compact
periodicity requires a mean-zero static contrast source; a universal pointwise
square-root force fails a curl test; and the finite regulator changes the
arbitrarily weak-source scaling. An equal-Newton-coupling family retains a
defective double zero-speed scalar root, so the paper does not claim complete
stability or a finished cosmology.

The revision separates action-level closure, dynamical stability, and
phenomenological success at the outset. It also states explicitly that the
reservoir is reversible and that no entropy-production, particle-production,
or thermodynamic-stability claim follows without additional constitutive data.

No observational preference, MOND acceleration normalization, universal 2/3
factor, irreversible entropy mechanism, or all-sector quantum closure is
claimed.
