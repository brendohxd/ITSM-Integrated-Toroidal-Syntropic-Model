# R4C1 revision notes — v0.1.1-conditional-draft

This revision incorporates the recorded manuscript critique without changing
the declared action, registered domains, gate classifications, or numerical
receipts.

## Changes

- The abstract and introduction now foreground the three separate questions:
  action-level exchange closure, dynamical stability/well-posedness, and
  weak-field or cosmological phenomenology.
- Positive results are presented before the stability and matching boundaries,
  while retaining the exact finite-charge, conservation, and density-mode
  qualifications.
- The equal-Newton double zero-speed result is labelled as a boundary of the
  tested family rather than a universal statement about all extensions.
- The curl result is described as a conditional integrability obstruction to a
  pointwise shortcut, not as a no-go theorem for projected or nonlocal
  solutions.
- The reversible-reservoir assumption is self-contained: this paper makes no
  claim of positive entropy production, particle creation, thermodynamic
  stability, or an irreversible cosmological arrow.
- The falsification table is explicitly prospective and is not presented as a
  completed observational programme.

## Explicit non-changes

- No value of \(K_Q\), \(V\), \(a_0\), or the \(2/3\) coefficient is derived.
- No full interacting Hessian, strong-hyperbolicity domain, all-sector quantum
  closure, or action-to-observable map is promoted.
- Deferred Rule-9 review remains deferred; substantive physics holds remain
  active.
