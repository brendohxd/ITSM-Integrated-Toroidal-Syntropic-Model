# Paper 2 — Submission readiness

## Scope and honest product

This is a bounded negative-result technical paper. It tests an already-
renormalized nonzero-mode periodic-scalar stress on rectangular flat (T^3)
and transports that static stress through a declared instantaneous (a^{-4})
closure on a positive de Sitter biaxial Bianchi-I background.

The paper does not claim a general dynamical-QFT no-go theorem, a persistent
(13/12) prediction, a Hubble-tension resolution, a galactic acceleration
scale, or a complete compact-cosmology calculation.
