Cover letter / arXiv comment for Paper 2
========================================

Suggested primary target: arXiv (gr-qc, cross-list hep-th, astro-ph.CO)

Dear Editor / arXiv moderators,

Please find attached a technical paper on anisotropic Casimir stress for a free
massless scalar on a rectangular flat three-torus, and on free-field biaxial
backreaction of that stress.

The manuscript has a deliberately limited claim set:

  (1) We validate a renormalized lattice Casimir energy density and directional
      pressures (cutoff convergence, cubic isotropy, thermodynamic derivatives,
      conformal trace, dimensional scaling), establishing shape-dependent
      anisotropic vacuum stress as a concrete free-field mechanism.

  (2) Coupling that stress to biaxial Bianchi-I expansion on a positive de Sitter
      background, we search for the historically proposed ratio Ht/Hp = 13/12.
      In the free-field scan reported here the ratio appears at most as a brief
      transient; we find no quasi-plateau and no attractor. Every valid candidate
      returns to isotropy by late expansion.

We do not claim a resolution of the Hubble tension, a parameter-free prediction
H0 = 72.97 km/s/Mpc, a derivation of a galactic acceleration scale, or a
completed cubic cosmology compatible with Planck topology bounds. Persistent
anisotropy, if present in a fuller theory, requires a derived driven or memory
stress beyond free Casimir (open programme, not this paper).

Public code and stage summaries are in the companion repository under
Analysis/Casimir/CBR-001/.

Thank you for your consideration.
