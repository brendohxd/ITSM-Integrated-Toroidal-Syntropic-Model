Dear Editor / arXiv moderators,

Please find attached a short technical note on present-epoch scale matching
between the MOND acceleration a0 and c H0, together with the weak-field
coefficient C_obs in an AQUAL-type action.

The manuscript does not claim a resolution of the missing-mass problem, a new
complete cosmology, or a verdict on MOND as a class. Its purpose is to separate
postulates from derivations for an explicitly author-defined geometric hypothesis that
combines:

  (1) a cubic flat T^3 of side L = c/H0 with a putative circulation quantum
      kappa = c L, and
  (2) the isotropic projector ratio Tr(h)/Tr(gamma) = 2/3 with the deep-MOND
      coefficient C_obs.

The analysis finds that (1) is not circulation quantization, is kinematically
inconsistent when omega = 2 pi v/L is the cycle clock, and fails the cited
Planck cubic-topology benchmark under its stated background and statistics.
No centripetal acceleration is assigned to a flat torus geodesic.
Exact dynamical tracking L_phys(t) = c/H(t) is
incompatible with fixed comoving moduli in ordinary FLRW. The projector
identity (2) does not appear in the weak-field action and cannot fix C_obs; we
derive the field-rescaling invariant C_obs = C_m^{3/2}/sqrt(C_IR). If a0 is
also free, the spherical force identifies only C_obs^2 a0. In the
conditional static spherical baseline, imposing both a0 = c H0/(2 pi) and the
un-derived comparator C_obs = 2/3 gives a 4/9 effective normalization and an
approximately 18% asymptotic velocity shift. The manuscript explicitly does
not present this check as a global T^3 prediction or a completed data fit.

The note is intended as a clarifying contribution to the MOND / compact-topology
literature and as a checklist for any future geometric derivation. The author
used AI-assisted tools for editorial drafting, source-code assistance, and
symbolic/numerical consistency checks. Final author verification of the
content, sources, and artifacts and confirmation of responsibility remain
pending. This letter must not be sent until those steps and applicable review
are completed and the author explicitly approves submission.
