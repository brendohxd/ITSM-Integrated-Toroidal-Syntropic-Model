## SPARC interpretation and reproducibility boundary

The likelihood is the conditional algebraic simple-nu comparator with fixed
empirical a0=1.2e-10 m/s2 and C=1. It samples catalogue distance/inclination
errors and declared disk/applicable-bulge mass-to-light priors. It is not a
solved disk AQUAL field or an action-derived ITSM prediction. Simplified
geometry, independence/Gaussian assumptions, missing radial covariance and
gas-template uncertainty remain limitations. No Bayesian evidence, ITSM
model-preference statistic or posterior-predictive goodness of fit follows.

Four 64-walker ensembles per galaxy ran for 10,000 steps initially. The 54
unmet cases continued to 20,000 total steps without changing priors,
likelihood, membership or thresholds. The original 121 were preserved.
All remaining 18 miss Rhat<1.01; UGC00128 remains seriously unresolved and
UGC02953's Rhat worsened after continuation. More steps alone are not the
next general remedy. UGC01281's large stellar-mass-ratio support requirement
remains a model-domain/prior conflict, not evidence for that mass ratio.

Public checkpoint candidates include contracts, inputs/provenance,
diagnostics, job summaries, artifact hashes and bounded replay records.
Raw incremental NPZ chains/random-state blocks remain local and Git-ignored;
their manifests reference them but do not put them in this Git delivery.
A complete independent rerun of all 700 jobs has not been performed.
Diagnostics and bounded replay must not be described as such a rerun.
