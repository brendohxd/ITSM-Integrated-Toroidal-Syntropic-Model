## Scientific lessons and limits

1. **Prepared density dynamics agree with the full archived scalar runs.**
   [G3DF](../Gates/RES-001/RES001_R4C1_G3_FINITE_K_DENSITY_REPORT_2026-10-08.md)
   reconstructs the physical density on prepared solution planes.
   After matching the actual initial density phase, the largest registered
   k=80 phase error is 3.95813e-6. This is a normalized calculation-to-
   calculation comparison, not fractional astrophysical accuracy. The
   raw initial map mismatch is retained separately; no universal
   density-only closure or physical EFT window follows.

2. **The ordinary cubic scattering route has a bounded obstruction.**
   [G3V](../Gates/RES-001/RES001_R4C1_G3_COUPLED_VERTEX_REGULARITY_REPORT_2026-10-08.md)
   shows that the force cusp survives transverse metric/dust constraint
   reduction on the original homogeneous G3 background. The ordinary
   trilinear Taylor vertex does not exist there. The
   [supplemental cross-check](../Gates/RES-001/RES001_R4C1_G3_VERTEX_REGULARITY_CROSSCHECK_REPORT_2026-10-08.md)
   and [source-integrity addendum](../Gates/RES-001/RES001_R4C1_G3V_SOURCE_INTEGRITY_ADDENDUM_2026-10-08.md)
   preserve the original sealed result and distinguish the later audit.
   This does not reject every classical evolution, other background or
   nonanalytic interaction prescription.

3. **Nonanalyticity alone does not forbid the frozen classical probe.**
   [G3N1](../Gates/RES-001/RES001_R4C1_G3N1_CLASSICAL_CUSP_AMPLITUDE_REPORT_2026-10-08.md)
   gives a regular Hamiltonian and unique global evolution for its
   declared frozen-coefficient amplitude ODE. Its force generates a
   nonzero third harmonic, so a single Fourier mode is not an invariant
   nonlinear field subspace. Full coupled PDE, evolving-background,
   regulator, scattering and quantum consistency are still open.

4. **Nearby nonzero-gradient homogeneous candidates are now explicit.**
   [G3T](../Gates/RES-001/RES001_R4C1_G3_TILTED_BACKGROUND_REPORT_2026-10-08.md)
   supplies 24 reduced constraint-compatible initial states and eight
   short local evolutions retaining the smooth positive-gradient patch,
   positive dust density, and energy/charge checks. It is a reduced
   homogeneous result, not a complete off-ansatz four-dimensional audit
   or a declaration that the backgrounds are physically healthy.

5. **The coupled scalar clock/domain question is now executable.**
   [G3K](../Gates/RES-001/RES001_R4C1_G3_FINITE_K_SCALAR_REPORT_2026-10-08.md)
   retains metric, frame, condensate, reservoir, force, regulator, dust
   and longitudinal-shift couplings for propagation along the tilt.
   It keeps all coefficient time derivatives. The exact shift denominator
   is A_B=-eta V(1-5w^2)k^2/(36 N a_parallel^2). k=0 and w^2=1/5 are
   never inverted. Smaller registered tilts give kinetic inertia
   (6 positive,1 negative,0 zero); w=1/2 gives (5,2,0).
   All roots, including positive frozen real exponents, are retained.
   These findings require a preferred-Cauchy/characteristic and constraint-
   chart analysis; they are not a physical ghost/instability theorem.

6. **A small background deviation is not automatically a weak interaction.**
   [G3I](../Gates/RES-001/RES001_R4C1_G3_FRAME_SCATTERING_REPORT_2026-10-08.md)
   derives a fixed-metric vacuum probe amplitude proportional to
   p^2/eta and an order-one probe momentum proportional to sqrt(eta).
   Its endpoint is nonuniform. This diagnostic does not supply the full
   finite-density physical cutoff or exclude every GR approach.
