## Scope and first deciding questions

Compare F-tau (whole frame restricted before variation) and R-tau (only the
regulator follows the dust clock). The first bundle establishes:

1. The off-shell clock variation and corresponding additional matter-exchange
   term introduced by each candidate. Check signs and dimensions.
2. What happens to the auxiliary velocity pairing in the actual clock-gauge
   action and nonzero-mode constraint reduction.
3. The full reduced temporal kinetic block on the common admitted old-B1
   homogeneous control, and, for R-tau only, on the declared small transverse
   tilt diagnostic if its own constrained homogeneous admission is justified.

Do not claim a physical domain/window from these first tests. Full gradients,
characteristics, time-dependent persistence and interactions remain separate
requirements. A failed first test is a scoped disposition, not an all-action
no-go. No external model/reviewer dispatch.

Use the original physical-density contractions and unspecialized weak-action
backend, with explicit, audited modifications matching the two definitions.

own reduced temporal Hessian, constraint preservation and regular auxiliary

Derive or reject exact identities with previously exported positive blocks
only AFTER candidate-specific recomputation. An exact identity can reuse

## Controls and acceptance

- Freeze source hashes and candidate/contract hashes; preserve dirty/private
  work and prior failed attempts. Fresh versioned executables/receipts,
  non-overwriting output directories and exact read-only replay.
- Exact symbolic identities, symmetry and dimensions; verify the actual
  constraint eliminations and reject an omitted acceleration term or
  post-variation old-equation projection.
- Independent differentiation of the unmodified physical norm-cubed
  expression with the declared candidate replacement. At nonzero tilt use
  both signs, complex-step scales 1e-20/1e-26 and 120-digit arithmetic,
  comparing full derivative blocks with normalized error <1e-35.
- At exact alignment use real centered finite differences of the physical
  action, since a complex analytic Taylor assumption for Y^(3/2) at zero
  is invalid. Use scales 1e-10/1e-16 and normalized error <1000*step.
- Retain failure if these predeclared checks fail; repair implementation in
  preserved new versions, never loosen a threshold to obtain PASS.

Acceptance: explicit candidate-specific clock/exchange identities and an
actual bounded kinetic/constraint disposition with independently relevant
controls. Background admission, kinetic positivity, gradient health,
nonlinear preservation and a physical validity window are distinct claims.
