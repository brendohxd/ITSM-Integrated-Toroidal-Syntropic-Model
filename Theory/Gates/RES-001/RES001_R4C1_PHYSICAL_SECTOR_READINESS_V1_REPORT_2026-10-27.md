profile g=b*w^2 vanishes at two alignment nodes. The local scalar/regulator
inverse used away from those nodes is not automatically a global continuum
inverse. The required cross-corrected auxiliary range conditions, admissible
regularity and moving-background persistence must decide whether the warning
directions survive and how they are normalized. Do not confuse pointwise nodal
null rows with an already classified kernel on a chosen continuum function space.

instantaneous necessary-condition analysis; it does require the evidence needed
