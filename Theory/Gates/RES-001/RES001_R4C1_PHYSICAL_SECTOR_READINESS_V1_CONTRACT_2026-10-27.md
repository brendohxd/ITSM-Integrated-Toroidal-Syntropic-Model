## Bounded analysis and decision rules

1. Reconstruct the actual two-form and its scalar/regulator range conditions,
   including canonical crosses, from the supplied action/reduction. Distinguish
   the regular g>0 region from both g=0 traces and from the spatial zero mode.
2. State the admissible function space and regularity required to divide by g.
   Determine which conditions are known initial constraints, which follow from
   the linearized range condition, and which require unproved persistence.
   A finite-node Schur inverse or partial Legendre inverse is insufficient.
3. Where a valid reduction is available, derive the physical tangent map and
   quadratic/symplectic normalization. Track source covectors and retained matter,
   metric/frame and mean effects. A formal regular-patch Darboux map is not a
   global physical reduction. Do not call a coordinate Hessian eigenvalue a mode.
4. Determine admission/removal of the warning directions only if justified by
   that actual domain. If background or hierarchy evidence is essential and
   absent, specify the exact operator/equation and why existing receipts cannot
   supply it. Do not assume an exact evolving background or insert an arbitrary
   positive norm, regularize away a node, or extend an inverse through rank loss.
5. If full reduction succeeds, check its domain, kinetic signatures and principal
   evolution in this sector with an independently formulated control. If it does
   not, deliver the bounded obstruction, not a new unbounded consistency programme.

Accepted outcome: a scoped justified physical-sector result, demonstrated
incompatibility, or a precisely evidenced obstruction. In the latter case leave
admission, physical normalization and the stability/window questions OPEN. Missing
on-shell evolution alone does not prohibit useful instantaneous necessary-condition
analysis; it does prohibit claiming persistence or a physical spectrum without
the corresponding justification. No unrestricted instability/ghost verdict follows
from the negative clock-Hamiltonian restriction.

Any new computational claim requires a versioned executable, fresh receipt
directory, independent relevant control and immutable replay. A source inventory
or document hash check is not scientific validation. No heavy rerun of completed
initial/energy/seed audits is part of this task. If no new scientific executable
