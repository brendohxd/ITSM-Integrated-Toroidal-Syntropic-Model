## Proposed interface and decision

The smallest useful proposal is an explicit action-level bridge fixing the
temporal/spatial response of the field that changes the matter metric. Require
an independent dimensional scale and a symmetry/matching condition fixing the
physical coefficient relations, with ensemble and finite-density background.
The current unchanged R4C1 action does not supply those identifying relations.

Its matter-defined chart theta_m=beta*psi gives K_theta=K_Q/beta^2,
A_theta=A/beta^3 and b_theta=b/beta^2 on the positive-beta branch. The isolated
ratios beta/sqrt(K_Q), A/K_Q^(3/2) and b/K_Q are invariant under simultaneous
field redefinition. They are not thereby numerically determined, and the first
ratio is not yet the complete coupled physical-mode residue.

No new operator or symmetry is proposed as established here. This screen asks
whether the existing candidate inputs justify launching a new identifying
calculation now; they do not. It does not repeat the previous scientific audits.

## Concrete next-input proposal, before expensive reduction

A candidate must provide all of the following in one small submission:

1. Joint operators relating condensate, force mode and matter metric/mass response;
   state any added fields, broken/protected symmetries and charge consequences.
2. An independently justified physical scale and coefficient relation, with its
   actual source and uncertainty; no desired acceleration or Hubble target used
   to select it.
3. A controlled soft limit showing nonzero static matter overlap without silently
   gapping the required force mode; a time-derivative source alone is insufficient.
4. A remaining-parameter test: vary allowed coefficients at fixed claimed inputs.
   If either normalized residue or nonlinear invariant varies, identification has
   not succeeded. Then stop that numerical-prediction route explicitly.
5. A plan for the same-action constrained mode and nonlinear/regulator matching,
   including the finite-density background and domain. A full reduction is not
   authorized merely by writing this input list.

Until such new information is supplied or derived, no large new matching run is
justified by this screen. A calibrated EFT could use independently measured inputs
and reserve other data for predictions, but adopting that objective requires an
explicit operator decision. It cannot complete the theory-fixed numerical goal.
