## 1. Purpose

The minimal unwarped single-tension brane child has been conditionally
rejected. This contract records the only currently registered brane-family
continuation without silently changing X4-S2F3.

The existing stabilization-candidate audit already classified X4-S4 as an
orbifold/brane route whose fixed points change the geometry and require
localized actions, counterterms and junction conditions. It deferred that
route as a different parent. This handoff preserves that boundary.

This contract does not freeze X4-S4, select its fields, add a second brane,
choose a warp profile, derive a compensator, or calculate K_Q, V or a
physical Hessian.

## 2. Candidate-route inventory

### X4-S4: orbifold/brane new-parent design

This is the surviving brane-family route. A future X4-S4 design may use an
orbifold or another explicitly declared localized geometry, one or more
localized matter actions, and a bulk stabilization sector. It must derive:

- the new manifold, fixed-point/defect structure and spin structures;
- the complete bulk and localized action;
- induced metrics, localized stress and radion source;
- junction, embedding and brane-bending equations;
- the exact integrated compact-space balance;
- localized regulator/subtraction and counterterm conditions; and
- a finite-charge on-shell background before any H1 source projection.

X4-S4 is a new parent design, not an extension of the frozen X4-S2F3
control.

### X4-S3: one-dimensional smooth-circle flux repair

The registered audit found no distinct minimal smooth-S1 classical flux repair:
a two-form has no nonzero purely internal two-form component on a
one-dimensional circle, a flat Wilson line has no classical stress, and an
axion/phase one-form winding is already represented by the X4-S1 route.
Adding a new flux field or higher-dimensional internal cycle would be a new
parent design and must be registered as such.

### Local induced-curvature term alone

An induced four-dimensional curvature counterterm is required in a quantum
brane theory, but it does not by itself cancel a constant background tension
on a flat four-dimensional slice. It is a renormalized operator, not an
unproved global compensator.

### Hidden second brane or negative source

A second localized source may participate in a global balance, but it changes
the topology, defect content and junction system. It is part of a new
explicit X4-S4-like parent design and cannot be inserted as an unregistered
balancing term.
