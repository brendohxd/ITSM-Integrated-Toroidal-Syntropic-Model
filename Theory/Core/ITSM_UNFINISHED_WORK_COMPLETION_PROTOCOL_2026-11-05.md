# Systematic completion of unfinished work

5 November 2026. Operator correction to the chronology-audit workflow:
recover and complete unfinished pathways, rather than repeatedly assessing
whether each old tangent deserves attention. Proceed unless evidence gives
a substantive reason not to. This supplements the preserved audit plans;
their historical records and hashes remain unchanged.

## Execution rule

1. Extract the original unfinished deliverable and its acceptance test.
2. Check only the prerequisites and exclusions necessary to execute it safely
   and correctly. Reuse already-inspected evidence; no repeated portfolio debate.
3. Perform the next supported calculation/test and verify its actual result.
4. Continue its supported follow-ups automatically within the approved scope.
5. Mark complete only when the deliverable's acceptance test is satisfied.
   A rigorous negative calculation can complete a test without validating a model.

Operator suspension, supersession of a planning document, a lost execution
trail or a report explaining a pause DOES NOT complete the underlying task.
An audit note is not a substitute for the missing calculation. Completion of
an early task must not be conflated with completion of its entire parent route.

## Legitimate reasons not to proceed

- Existing verified work already satisfies the SAME deliverable and scope:
  link it, mark the task complete and avoid duplication.
- A demonstrated scientific exclusion defeats the task's actual premises or
  intended use: record the precise scope and affected follow-ups.
- A completed successor genuinely answers the original question: state the
  mapping, rather than relying on a shared name or newer date.
- A genuinely missing required input prevents execution: keep INPUT_BLOCKED
  with the exact input and supported next means of obtaining it. Do not call
  it completed, disproved or permanently retired.
- A new action, state, physical prescription, external publication, paid/model
  dispatch or other action outside existing authority is required: identify
  the concrete decision and continue independent authorized work meanwhile.

Lack of a positive result, an old priority change, review-only debt or the
existence of a newer interesting idea is not itself an exclusion. Neither
does this instruction authorize fabricating inputs, relaxing acceptance tests
or adopting a different physical parent silently.
