## 1. Binding interpretation

The Ban List separates a **research topic**, which may remain worth testing,
from a **claim formulation**, which may be forbidden because it outruns the
evidence. Reopening a topic does not clear its old slogan, coefficient, fit, or
mechanism as Derived.

The live boundary is:

- MAT-001 is `BLOCKED`; the invariant matter-to-physical-mode response is not
  computed in the live route.

  $C_{\rm obs}$ are not fixed by the ITSM parent action.
- SCR, LEN, DISK, STAT, COS, TOP, WAK, RES and ASTRO cannot inherit closure

## 4. Research protocol

1. Start from the current parent-gate status and declare whether work is
   Derived, Conditional, Open, Rejected, or a control.
2. Derive the physical degrees of freedom, normalized source response,
   stability domain and cutoff before restoring phenomenology.
3. Separate raw likelihood, priors, nuisance parameters, exclusions and
   numerical convergence. Never relabel optimization as posterior sampling.
4. Treat honest failure or a no-go result as a valid research outcome.
5. If Rule 9 review is used, seal the exact prompts, preserve role
   independence, and hash reports only after the reports exist. Consensus
   cannot overrule a failed parent checklist.
