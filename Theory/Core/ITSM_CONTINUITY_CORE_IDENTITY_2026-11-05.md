This compact packet is for continuity retrieval. It preserves model identity and
founding intent; it does not establish a derived physical result.

The founding question was whether an entropy-only description overlooks an
organizing counterpart or influence, called syntropy. Polarity and duality
motivated the question. Equal entropy cancellation, universal balance, or a
fixed entropy-to-syntropy ratio were never required by the original idea.

The wider identity has these linked blocks:

- an open thermodynamic circuit rather than a sealed cosmic battery;
- an active finite-density condensate or superfluid plenum;
- a compact toroidal or otherwise compact-flat boundary with global modes;
- fluid, acoustic, torsional or wake response to baryonic sources;
- winding, defects and resonance as a possible ordering principle;
- matter/plenum exchange and reservoir throughput, with total conservation
  separated from entropy production.

These blocks are related architecture, not six completed derivations. Syntropy is
broader than a bookkeeping current. A reservoir current is not automatically a
new force. Topology supplies boundary conditions and modes, not numerical
coefficients. Winding/resonance survives independently of the rejected SWNT
observational package. A wake survives independently of rejected static cluster
toys.

Safe status: identity or open hypothesis. Keep the physical question visible
while deriving mechanisms and observables under the owning gates. Do not silently
replace it with ordinary heat exchange, or promote it because a continuity record
was retrieved.
