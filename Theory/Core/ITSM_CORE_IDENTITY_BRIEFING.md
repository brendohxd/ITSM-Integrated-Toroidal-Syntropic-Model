## Core proposition

ITSM is a research programme testing whether an active finite-density vacuum,
compact toroidal topology, open-system thermodynamics, and a causal
low-acceleration response can be embedded in one covariant, stable, and
falsifiable framework. The recovery programme preserves those questions while
requiring every proposed mechanism to earn its status from a declared action and
reproducible evidence.

## Identity pillars under recovery

1. **Active vacuum / plenum:** retained as a physical postulate. Its complete
   microscopic force sector remains open.
2. **Open thermodynamic circuit:** exchange with a reservoir may be studied, but
   the complete declared system must satisfy the relevant conservation laws.
3. **Compact toroidal topology:** `T^3`, twists, moduli, winding, and defects are
   research structures, not automatic numerical predictions.
4. **Wake and memory:** causal relaxation or retarded response remains a
   candidate mechanism until a covariant constitutive closure is demonstrated.
5. **Emergent low-acceleration response:** AQUAL-class behaviour is a
   Conditional weak-field route. Its normalization is not Derived until the
   microscopic matter-to-mode residue and kinetic normalization are matched.
