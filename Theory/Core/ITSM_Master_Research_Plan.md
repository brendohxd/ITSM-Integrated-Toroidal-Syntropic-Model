## 1. Governing rule

> **Identity first → derive mechanisms second → restore predictions only afterward.**

Never reverse that order to chase a better fit, a striking number, or packaging
that outruns the field sector (e.g. promoting an IR coefficient to “derived”
before MAT-001 / matching is real). **Identity pillars and untested formal
routes stay available for development**; what is forbidden is claiming them as
**Derived** under failed packaging.

**Corollary:** no observational claim may outrank its **upstream field sector**.

**Open-options rule (do not over-lock):**

| May do anytime | Must not do |
|----------------|-------------|
| Formalize or sketch **identity pillars** as Conditional/Open (syntropy, toroid, wake, SWNT *principle*, twisted BC, reservoir law, alternate force completions) | Treat an untested route as **hard-banned** because recovery has not run it yet |
| Work **parallel** gates (TOP / VOR / WAK / constitutive \(Q_{\rm syn}\)) while UVIR-003 is critical path | Treat UVIR-only as the *only* allowed research |
| When a core gate stalls, **look harder** for missing structure among identity pillars (including winding/SWNT *principle*) before abandoning the pillar set | Force any one pillar (incl. SWNT) if math shows it is wrong or unused |
| Reopen Bucket B/C topics under a **new gate** with new math | Re-publish old slogans as Derived (Bucket A packaging) |
| Use AQUAL-class IR as Conditional baseline for honest fits | Replace whole identity stack with pure MOND packaging |

**Statuses (exactly one per claim):**

| Label | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Derived** | Checked calculation from declared assumptions in a stated domain |
| **Conditional** | Requires a named postulate, matching hypothesis, or restricted limit |
| **Open** | Defined calculation or test incomplete — **default for untested routes** |
| **Rejected** | Stated *derivation form* failed; not live support under that form (may return only with a new gate + new derivation — not by slogan) |

## 2. Ideal identity (what ITSM is for)

ITSM is a **research-stage layered effective framework**, not a finished alternative cosmology and not a free-parameter-free theory of everything.

The **original intuitive building blocks** that started the project — open dual
polarity, toroidal circuit, fluid/wake response, and winding–resonance structure
(including the SWNT *principle*) — remain the identity core. Recovery demotes
**false packaging** of those blocks, not the blocks themselves.

### 2.1 Physical proposition

The observable vacuum is an **active finite-density condensate** (plenum) whose:

- low-energy excitations (IR phonons / force sector),  
- global circulation and winding,  
- compact **\(T^3\)** (or twisted flat) boundary conditions,  
- exchanges with **matter** and a **reservoir**,  

may have gravitational consequences.

### 2.2 Ontological pillars (identity, not automatic coefficients)

These are co-equal founding blocks. Formal homes name where rigor lives; they
do **not** mean “park until UVIR finishes” or “abandon if numbers failed once.”

| Pillar | Content | Formal home | Status |
|--------|---------|-------------|--------|
| **Open thermodynamics** | Cosmos is not assumed to be a closed steam engine; dual polarity **entropy (exhaust) / syntropy (intake)** | Reservoir + \(Q_{\rm syn}^\nu\); total system conserved | **Identity — Keep** |
| **Toroidal shape** | Circuit geometry: compact flat \(T^3\); twisted \(E_2\)/\(E_3\) closer to recirculating stabilisers than pure cubic \(E_1\) alone | TOP-001; BC Conditional | **Identity — Open preference** |
| **Fluid plenum / wake** | Galactic “DM”-like response as continuous drag / acoustic–torsional wake, not collisionless particles first | \(\psi\) + WAK-001 | **Identity — Keep** |
| **Winding / resonance (SWNT principle)** | Ordered structure via quantized circulation, defects, and resonance — the *idea* that launched Phase-2 SWNT work | VOR-001 + condensate \(\Phi\) | **Identity — Keep principle** |
| **Minimal-coupling GR split** | Spin-2 on EH null cone when plenum is extra minimally coupled stress (GW170817-style) | Constraint on action; LEN/SCR | **Hard design constraint** |

**SWNT clarity (do not force):** the Phase-2 **principle** (winding, circulation
quanta, resonance ordering in a finite-density plenum) is a founding intuition
worth keeping **Open** under VOR-001. It is **not** a mandatory reintroduction
of the old SWNT programme, lunar proxy, or broken numbers. Use it as one place
to look harder when a core route hits a wall — same spirit as other identity
pillars — and drop or demote it if a better derivation shows it is not needed.

### 2.3 What ideal identity is *not* (live)

- Topology-alone derivation of \(a_0\) or \(C_{\rm obs}\)  
- Free-field Casimir attractor \(H_t/H_p=13/12\) or \(H_0=72.97\) as parameter-free prediction  
- Global “zero free parameters” / “all tensions resolved”  
- SPARC as independent cosmic \(H_0\)  
- Finished multi-tension ΛCDM replacement without closed gates  

**Phenomenological allowance:** \(a_0 \equiv cH_0/2\pi\) only as a **named present-epoch DSM / scale-matching postulate**, not as circulation quantization.

heading. Under the **ideal-identity + multi-route** scope (this plan v1.x), use
three buckets:

| Bucket | Meaning | How to use |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **A — Hard ban** | Proven false packaging, fabrication, or governance failure | Never as live Derived claim |

**Not Derived ≠ Hard ban.** Many identity pillars (syntropy, wake, winding principle, AQUAL IR, twisted topology) are Conditional/Open — they are **not** on the hard ban list.
