## What the completed tests decide

The unchanged R4C1 old homogeneous B1 control remains a valid full coupled
linear testbed. The following proposed ways of turning it into a healthy
nonlinear physical domain have not been established:

1. An unrestricted dust-clock neighbourhood admitting the registered small
   homogeneous tilts fails the positive-kinetic test in its declared scope.
2. Arbitrary exactly dust-aligned linear perturbations are not invariant.
3. Adding the first explicit alignment compatibility equation still does
   not give an invariant linear candidate: a pure condensate-amplitude seed
   satisfies it but leaves it under the actual time-dependent equations.

The later vortical eta=1/4 preparation has different parameters and its own
principal, Frobenius, forced-tail and weak-interface/concentration holds.

These results do not exhaust all same-action sectors, parameter families,

## Recommended decision

Do not spend the next substantial calculation on an additional arbitrary
initial compatibility row or another higher-precision propagator.

The most useful next selection is between (A) a specified unchanged-action
invariant-domain construction and (B) a bounded, explicitly unadopted
comparison of concrete action-level repairs. Recommend B if no defensible
candidate/input for A is available. The recommendation is based on the
demonstrated rank/kinetic and preservation mechanism, not a proof that repair
will succeed. The operator must select before a new action is varied,
implemented or adopted.
