# Ultra plan — reservoir action/open-EFT derivation

**Required reasoning:** Ultra  
**Dependency-locked:** yes  
**Entry condition:** a surviving parent fixes the plenum variables, physical
modes and stress tensor; the High RES scaffold audit is complete  
**Gate effect:** none until signed RES review  

## Purpose

Determine whether a microscopic system-reservoir model or controlled open EFT
can derive a covariant exchange current. This is not a continuation of the
finite-dimensional GKSL demonstration unless the physical map is established.

## R-0 — microscopic declaration

Specify:

- system and reservoir Hilbert variables;
- covariant/local operators and state;
- Hamiltonian/action, spectral density and coupling dimensions;
- stress-tensor definition;
- initial-state, coarse-graining, Markov and secular assumptions;
- zero-coupling/null theory.

## R-1 — controlled reduction

Derive the reduced dynamics through a declared influence functional,
Schwinger-Keldysh/open-EFT construction or justified master-equation limit.
Demonstrate the approximation domain and retain noise/dissipation relations
required by the chosen state.

## R-2 — covariant exchange current

- vary the off-shell action/effective action with respect to the metric;
- derive system, reservoir and matter stress tensors;
- derive the exchange current(s) and Ward identities;
- demonstrate total conservation;
- show explicitly that the separate `Q^mu` is supplied by the interaction, not
  invented from the Bianchi identity.

## R-3 — physical checks

- positivity/complete positivity in the applicable limit;
- entropy production and fluctuation-dissipation consistency;
- positive sector densities in the stated domain;
- absence of singular transfer coefficients;
- stable background and perturbation eigenmodes;
- continuous zero-coupling and GR limits;
- cutoff and memory/nonlocality bounds.

## R-4 — cosmological interface

Only for a survivor, derive background and linear-perturbation transfer terms,
including momentum exchange. Do not fit `Q proportional (1+z)^(-n)` or open a
likelihood in this plan.

## Exit classifications

- controlled derived open-EFT current;
- phenomenological closure with explicit assumptions;
- no-go/incompatible approximation domain;
- incomplete microscopic map.

## Cost boundary

Stop after each of R-1, R-2 and R-3 for High review. Do not combine this goal
with the physical `T^3` spectrum or cosmological likelihood work.
