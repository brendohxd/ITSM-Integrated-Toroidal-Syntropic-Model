# Ultra plan — physical `T^3` spectrum from a surviving parent

**Required reasoning:** Ultra  
**Dependency-locked:** yes  
**Entry conditions:** the coupled parent survives the Ultra residue plan; the
winding convention audit and Max winding-template calculation are complete;
one common parent action is frozen  
**Gate effect:** none until signed TOP/VOR review  

## Purpose

Derive the compact spectrum of the actual constrained physical modes. This
replaces conditional Bogoliubov box examples with a parent-specific result, if
the required join exists.

## T-0 — common parent and background

- declare rectangular `T^3` moduli and boundary conditions;
- place the winding sector on the same on-shell finite-density parent;
- solve all background metric, amplitude, phase and support equations;
- treat zero modes and global charge constraints explicitly;
- forbid `L=c/H`, `2*pi`, winding number or a target PTA band as normalization
  inputs.

## T-1 — constrained compact modes

- expand the full coupled action about the compact/winding background;
- eliminate local constraints without discarding global modes;
- derive the physical amplitude-phase-metric eigenoperator;
- calculate eigenfrequencies, residues, polarizations and degeneracies;
- track rectangular-modulus and winding dependence.

## T-2 — mathematical and numerical validation

- analytic low-mode checks;
- spectral or finite-difference calculation at multiple truncations;
- convergence and regulator tests;
- reflection/permutation symmetries;
- zero-winding, isotropic and decompactification limits;
- comparison with the earlier VOR templates without inheritance of their
  coefficients or statuses.

## T-3 — observable-coupling decision

Determine which physical modes couple to matter, photons, GWs or the reservoir
and with what normalized amplitude. A frequency alone is not a PTA/GW
prediction. Do not compare with data until propagation and source production
are derived.

## Exit classifications

- physical parent-specific compact spectrum;
- mathematical spectrum with no derived observable coupling;
- template-only result because the parent join fails;
- scoped compact/winding instability or no-go.

## Cost boundary

Stop after T-1 and T-2 for High review. Any PTA, CMB or stochastic-background
likelihood requires a new downstream plan and explicit authorization.
