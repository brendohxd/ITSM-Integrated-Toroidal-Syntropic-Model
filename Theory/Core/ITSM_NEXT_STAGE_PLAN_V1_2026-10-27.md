## What the latest results change

1. Prepared dust has controlled finite-mode results only in its registered linear
   scope. First-material-order seed compatibility is useful new evidence, but
   neither result proves full constraint persistence or physical stability.
2. The negative scalar-rate curvature is a warning, not a demonstrated admitted
   ghost or exponential instability. Actual constraints and normalization decide
   its physical interpretation. An arbitrary positive weight cannot cure it.
3. The P0-B metric-host control avoids a separate direct matter-force residue but
   introduces a conditional high-acceleration constant-force tail and planetary
   restrictions. Its full physical window remains unresolved. It is not an
   automatically better replacement, nor has every PKM1 architecture been excluded.
4. R4C1's finite-regulator fixed-scale weak-source response becomes linear as
   source amplitude vanishes. A universal square-root asymptote is unavailable
   in that limit; any finite intermediate regime needs an explicit physical domain.
5. Neither normalization conventions nor assumed coefficients identify the matter
   residue and nonlinear force invariant. Free coefficients can define a useful
   EFT, but cannot be presented as a theory-fixed numerical prediction.

## Execution sequence for the proposed goal

### A. Short entry check and frozen physical question — Medium

Reuse the retained action, exact implicit initial constraints, source bundle,
material-seed and energy results. Inventory the actual auxiliary constraints,
gauge freedoms, fixed charges, torus means and background dependence needed for
a physical reduction. Select one registered branch/background and nonzero-mode
sector; keep the zero mode as a separate required disposition. Do not assume a
sampled background with a nonzero defect is an exact on-shell solution.

Deliver a short frozen contract before substantial algebra. State the precise
background/on-shell or controlled-residual requirements, operators available,
rank assumptions and which question the calculation can answer. If those inputs
are absent, stop at the named missing input with its required construction and
scope; do not launch a surrogate computation or repeat a completed initial audit.

### B. One R4C1 physical-admissibility/normalization calculation — High where needed

From the same action, derive the constraint-admissible perturbation space and
reduced quadratic/symplectic structure where justified. Retain moving-constraint
and background two-form drift, lapse/shift and matter effects relevant to that
sector; preserve ensemble, boundary and mean conditions. Derive the evolution
operator on that space rather than projecting every step onto a desired graph.

Determine whether the warning directions survive, are removed, or remain
undecidable. Calculate their kinetic signatures and principal characteristics
when a physical reduction is available. Treat rank-changing points separately;
do not continue an inverse through a singular constraint block.

Acceptance is one of:

- A valid reduced physical-sector result with explicit background/domain,
  constraints, normalization and limitations. A healthy result supports only
  its demonstrated sector; it is not a full coupled stability/window pass.
- A demonstrated incompatibility or pathological admitted mode in the declared
  scope, with an independently checked action-to-mode chain.
- A precise obstruction: identify the unsupplied operator/background condition,
  rank/persistence problem or uncontrolled remainder and why it prevents this
  classification. An extra initial-time derivative or numerical residual alone
  is not a substitute result.

Use exact identities and one independently formulated bounded numerical or
analytic control suited to the claim. Preserve failures and new versioned receipts.
After this result, STOP AND ASSESS. No automatic higher-order hierarchy, full QR
continuation, enlarged parameter survey, new parent or TOP-X4 pivot.

### C. One matching-input screen — Medium, High only for a new derivation

Prepare a small proposal specifying the independent physical information that
could fix both the normalized static matter response and nonlinear force invariant.
Use the existing RM1/ST1/ST2 outcomes; do not repeat their completed screens.
Show the action/operator bridge, physical scale, ensemble and remaining free
parameters. Test whether allowed parameter changes preserve the supplied inputs
but change the claimed residue or nonlinear normalization.

If no new identifying information exists, deliver that explicit result and keep
the numerical-prediction objective incomplete. Do not invent a microscopic parent.
Any new fields/operators require a separate action-selection decision. A calibrated
EFT is a legitimate alternative objective, but requires the operator to choose it
and must distinguish calibration data from held-out predictions.

### D. Independent paper lane — Medium

Use current P1 0.2.0-rc7 and P2 0.2.0-rc4. Produce one concise list of outstanding
author proofread/declaration, review, metadata and venue/deposit decisions. Reuse
existing builds and audits; reopen edits only for a specific material defect.
Repository records do not by themselves prove the current external deposit state.

Keep release order P1/P2, then Thermodynamics 0.7.1-conditional-draft and R4C1
0.1.1-conditional-draft as independently scoped notes. Thermodynamics and R4C1
may exchange order if their focused review/readiness warrants it. Preserve P2's

plan, same-author checks or deferred review certifies journal readiness.
