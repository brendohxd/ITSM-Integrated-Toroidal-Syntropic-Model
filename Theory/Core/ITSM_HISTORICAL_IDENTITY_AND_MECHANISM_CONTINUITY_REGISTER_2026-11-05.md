# ITSM historical identity and mechanism continuity register

Status: working recovery register, recorded 5 November 2026.

This document answers a continuity question: which ideas gave ITSM its identity,
what role did each idea originally play, what later packaging failed or diluted
it, and where can the idea be carried forward without silently upgrading it to a
scientific result? It is a navigation and provenance document. It is not a new
action, gate closure, derivation, or publication claim.

## Why this register exists

The historical archive was created to prevent both kinds of loss: allowing a
rejected numerical claim to return without its failure reason, and allowing a
useful physical question to disappear merely because one attempted formulation
failed. The archive reports 453 unique documents, with 334 individually read and
119 scoped as infrastructure or data rather than narrative claims. This register
adds an identity-level map above the claim ledger so that a future session can
recover the purpose of a route before reading its latest result.

The governing distinction is:

> A failed package, coefficient, fit, or derivation is not automatically a
> failed physical question. A preserved idea is not automatically a derived
> physical claim.

## Recovery categories

- **Identity — Keep:** the idea belongs to the model's motivating architecture.
- **Open mechanism:** the question remains scientifically unresolved and may be
  formalized under its owning gate.
- **Conditional route:** useful for scoped effective or phenomenological work,
  with assumptions and free inputs declared.
- **Packaging rejected:** a specific numerical or rhetorical formulation failed;
  it must not return under the same wording.
- **Historical lead only:** a thread is preserved for traceability but lacks
  enough evidence for current scientific use.
- **Hard design constraint:** a compatibility requirement that any surviving
  action must satisfy.

These categories must remain separate from the exact claim statuses `Derived`,
`Conditional`, `Open`, and `Rejected` used by the scientific ledger.

## What must not be silently collapsed again

1. Syntropy as the founding hypothesis is broader than an entropy ledger or a
   transfer current.
2. A reservoir exchange current is not automatically a new force, and total
   stress-energy conservation is not an entropy law.
3. A toroidal boundary supplies geometry, modes and possible stress; it does not
   by itself determine `2/3`, `13/12`, `a_0`, or a BTFR normalization.
4. The winding/resonance principle survives independently of the rejected SWNT
   observational package.
5. A fluid or wake ontology survives independently of the rejected static Bullet
   toy models.
6. A successful calculation on an old or specialized operator does not clear the
   current action, its matching, or its physical validity window.
7. A dropped historical route must receive `Keep`, `Formalize`, or `Reject with
   reason`; silence is not a scientific disposition.
