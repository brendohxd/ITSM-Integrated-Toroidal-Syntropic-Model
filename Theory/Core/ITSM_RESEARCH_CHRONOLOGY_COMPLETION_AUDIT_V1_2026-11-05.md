# ITSM research chronology and completion audit - V1

5 November 2026. Operator requested a planned chronology checklist to check
whether promising preceding work was left behind as newer ideas developed.
This is an audit plan with an initial evidence map, not a completed historical
audit, a new scientific result or a replacement of G1-G6.

## Purpose and boundaries

Identify what was achieved, why attention moved, and what remains worth
finishing. Older work is not presumed better; newer work is not presumed
necessary merely because it has accumulated many receipts. Preserve negative
results and distinguish a useful scoped result from a complete physical theory.

The immediate work order is this portfolio audit before starting another hard
density derivation. Preserve the prepared R-tau handoff. Routine audit work is
suitable for Medium; request High before a new difficult scientific derivation.
This plan does not resume/replace the native goal, adopt an action, reopen a
gate, submit papers, dispatch reviewers, mutate the vault, commit or push.

## Evidence labels and route dispositions

Coverage: INDEX_FOUND, OWNER_READ, ARTIFACT_CHECKED, or REPLAYED. Reading a
heading or passing a hash check is not full scientific verification. Record
the actual coverage and date for each conclusion. Do not rerun expensive
calculations unless a concrete unresolved issue requires it.

Disposition: COMPLETE_SCOPED, RELEASE_PENDING, CALCULATION_OPEN,
INPUT_BLOCKED, SCIENTIFIC_FAILURE_SCOPED, REVIEW_DEBT_ONLY,
OPERATOR_SUSPENDED, SUPERSEDED_WITH_REASON, or UNRECONCILED.
More than one can apply: a calculation may be COMPLETE_SCOPED while a larger
physical claim remains INPUT_BLOCKED. Unclear status stays UNRECONCILED.

  matching, and seek authorization before changing that objective.

  of negative results. A scoped failure is not an all-ITSM no-go.
- [ ] Rank the resulting tasks by scientific decision value, finishability,
  independent usefulness and effort; do not assign invented precision scores.
- [ ] Recommend one substantive calculation at a time plus a small independent
  release/diagnostic task. Reassess new repairs against the preceding portfolio.
- [ ] Present the evidence and recommendation before changing the native goal
  or adopting an alternative action. No automatic publication or Git delivery.

## Audit completion criteria

Every identified lane and explicit historical follow-up has an evidence-backed
disposition or a named coverage gap. Every apparent dropped item is resolved,
retained as open, or explicitly suspended with a reason. The report separates
owner statements, inspected artifacts, replays, inference and unknowns.
Missing archives, inaccessible vault records and unreviewed branches prevent
any blanket claim that nothing was lost. Completing this audit is not completion
of G1-G6 or scientific clearance of the proposed model.
