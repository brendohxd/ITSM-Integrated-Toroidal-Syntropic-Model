## 1. BBN nuisance parameters in DESI–CMB inference

**Source/date:** Akharman et al., submitted 10 September 2026,
[arXiv:2609.12065](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12065).  
**Quality:** Unreviewed 29-page methodological preprint prepared for *JCAP*;
uses Planck and DESI DR2 data.

**Reported evidence.** The paper presents explicit marginalization over BBN
nuisance parameters for BAO+BBN and BAO+BBN+CMB combinations. Its fiducial
values include $h=0.6833^{+0.0048}_{-0.0053}$ for BAO+BBN and
$h=0.6823^{+0.0027}_{-0.0026}$ after adding CMB data. Allowing $N_eff$
to vary still gives $h≈0.68$, while nuclear and abundance inputs affect
the joint inference.

**ITSM boundary.** This constrains the pre-recombination sector: an effective
radiation or plenum component cannot be treated as an $H_0$-raising
mechanism without propagating its BBN consequences into the likelihood.

**Exact revision task.** Couple the early-plenum background and $Q^μ$ to a
BBN network such as PArthENoPE or AlterBBN. Marginalize neutron lifetime,
nuclear rates, D/H and $Y_p$, then fit Planck+DESI DR2 jointly over
$N_eff$, the plenum fraction and transfer parameters. Report the abundance
likelihoods together with the resulting $r_s$ and $H_0$.

## 2. Empirical helium as an independent early-time test

**Source/date:** Loverde, Saravanan & Weiner, submitted 11 September 2026,
[arXiv:2609.13140](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.13140).  
**Quality:** Unreviewed analysis of a recent Large Binocular Telescope
primordial-helium measurement.

**Reported evidence.** The LBT helium calibration permits CMB constraints that
are agnostic about standard-BBN closure while remaining highly informative.
The authors test interacting and free-streaming radiation, changing radiation
density, light relics and varying constants; the helium result excludes most,
but not every, tested Hubble-tension model through its BBN effects. It favours
the bottle neutron-lifetime result over beam measurements by $2.4σ$.

**ITSM boundary.** This challenges the completeness of the current ITSM early-
time treatment but is neutral on eventual viability. Direct empirical helium
constraints must be kept separate from an internally imposed standard-BBN
closure.

**Exact revision task.** Derive $Y_p$ and D/H as functions of the ITSM
BBN-era plenum density, $G_eff(z)$, $Q^μ$ and expansion rate. Fit
the LBT helium likelihood independently of standard-BBN closure and predeclare
the maximum allowed plenum fraction at $z≈10^9$.

## 3. JWST clustering halo-mass targets through (z=10)

**Source/date:** Lim et al., submitted 10 September 2026 to *MNRAS*,
[arXiv:2609.12040](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12040).  
**Quality:** Unreviewed, simulation-validated methodology applied to JADES
observations.

**Reported evidence.** Matching galaxy and halo two-point clustering over
($0.5<r_p/cMpc<1$) recovers halo masses with under 0.3-dex validation
scatter through $z=12$. For $M_UV<-17$, JADES gives
$log_{10}(M_h/M☉)=10.52^{+0.12}_{-0.21}$ and
$b_h=4.2^{+0.26}_{-0.41}$ at $z=6$, and
$log_{10}(M_h/M☉)=9.91^{+0.19}_{-0.34}$ and
$b_h=7.7^{+0.77}_{-1.16}$ at $z=10$. Field variance contributes
0.3–0.5-dex halo-mass uncertainty, and current data do not distinguish the
tested star-formation models.

**ITSM boundary.** This challenges claims of unique JWST support and supplies
growth targets; it does not itself select an ITSM growth mechanism.

**Exact revision task.** Generate ITSM light cones matching the JADES
selections, geometry and survey volumes. Fit the same two-point clustering
range and report $M_h$, $b_h$, cosmic variance and assembly-bias sensitivity
against ΛCDM and bursty-galaxy controls.

## 4. Bright and dark sirens

**Source/date:** Colombo, Triscari-Benimavò & Mastrogiovanni, submitted 10
September 2026,
[arXiv:2609.12053](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12053).  
**Quality:** Unreviewed three-author forecast; not an official LVK analysis.

**Reported evidence.** A small bright-siren sample can break the
$H_0$–$Ω_m$ degeneracy affecting dark sirens. Dark sirens alone reach
comparable discrimination only with an external $Ω_m$ prior. The paper
also introduces a shared-population framework for bright and dark events.

**ITSM boundary.** This methodologically constrains the
$a_0=C_χ cH_0$ coefficient audit: an $Ω_m$ prior inferred under
ΛCDM cannot be imported unaltered. This calibration remains
separate from GW friction and GW170817 speed, dispersion and arrival-time
tests.

**Exact revision task.** Construct a joint hierarchical bright–dark-siren
likelihood with shared compact-object population parameters. Infer $H_0$ and
$Ω_m$ within ITSM before propagating the posterior into $a_0$.

## 5. LVK limits on primordial-curvature enhancements

**Source/date:** An et al., submitted 11 September 2026,
[arXiv:2609.12867](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12867).  
**Quality:** Unreviewed six-author reanalysis of LVK O1–O4a data; not an
official collaboration result.

**Reported evidence.** The paper finds no evidence for scalar-induced
stochastic backgrounds from the tested ultra-slow-roll or inflationary phase-
transition scenarios and derives 95% upper limits on the primordial-curvature
spectrum across the ground-based frequency band, including regimes where
corresponding PBHs are too rare to detect or have evaporated.

**ITSM boundary.** This is neutral unless ITSM predicts enhanced primordial
curvature or a scalar-induced background; it becomes a constraint once such a
prediction is derived.

**Exact revision task.** Calculate the ITSM primordial scalar spectrum and its
induced $Ω_GW(f)$. Apply the O1–O4a likelihood to the complete
spectral shape and state which topology or plenum-transition parameter ranges
are excluded. Keep this distinct from late-time plenum backgrounds and
propagation constraints.

## 6. Intrinsic alignments in nonlinear weak-lensing statistics

**Source/date:** Chawla et al., submitted 10 September 2026,
[arXiv:2609.12038](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12038).  
**Quality:** Unreviewed Stage-IV weak-lensing simulation forecast; not
observational data.

**Reported evidence.** The simulations find that void statistics can improve
modified-gravity constraints by up to an order of magnitude over two-point
functions. Unmodelled galaxy intrinsic alignments cause major cosmological-
parameter biases and can sometimes mimic lower $Ω_m$ and $S_8$ without
an obviously poor fit.

**ITSM boundary.** This methodologically constrains Euclid/LSST validation. A
low inferred (S_8) cannot be attributed to plenum dynamics unless alignment
contamination has been marginalized.

**Exact revision task.** Add intrinsic-alignment fields to ITSM weak-lensing
mocks and jointly fit power spectra, PDFs, peaks, minima and void statistics.
Report whether ITSM’s inferred (S_8) and shear parameters remain stable across
alignment prescriptions and goodness-of-fit tests.
